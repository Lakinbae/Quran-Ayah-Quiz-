Pro Hifz Framework

Follow this daily 4-step routine to build a rock-solid memory:

1. Sabaq (New Memorization)

Memorize 3 to 5 new ayahs per day during peak focus hours (e.g., right after Fajr). Use the HQ audio to perfect your pronunciation.

2. Sabqi (Recent Revision)

Review the last 1-2 Juz currently being memorized to anchor them securely in your long-term memory.

3. Manzil (Old Revision)

Cycle through your previously memorized Juz. Aim to recite at least 1 Juz per day to ensure complete retention.

4. Active Drill Test

Run 15–20 questions in the Ayah Quiz app using the Mistake Drills and Juz Filters to identify and fix your weak spots.
